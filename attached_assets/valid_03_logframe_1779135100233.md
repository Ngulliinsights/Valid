# VALID — Logical Framework (Logframe)
**Version:** April 2026 | **Programme Period:** Phase 3 → Year 3 (2026–2028)
**Scope:** US Launch (Track B) primary; Kenya Pilot (Track A) conditional

---

## How to Read This Logframe

| Column | Definition |
|---|---|
| **Hierarchy of Objectives** | What the programme aims to achieve, from broad to specific |
| **Objectively Verifiable Indicators (OVIs)** | Specific, measurable evidence that the level has been achieved |
| **Means of Verification (MoV)** | Where/how the indicator is measured |
| **Assumptions** | Conditions outside programme control that must hold true for the level above to be achieved |

Rows are organised from bottom (inputs) to top (goal/impact), following the "if...then" logic of the programme.

### LEVEL 5 — GOAL (Impact)

**Goal Statement:** Informal supporters of people with mental illness consistently respond in ways that maintain relationships, reduce harm, and improve recovery outcomes — at population scale.

| Objectively Verifiable Indicators | Means of Verification | Assumptions |
|---|---|---|
| OVI 5.1: Measurable reduction in average time-to-formal-care in communities where VALID is deployed (vs. comparison group), assessed at Year 5 | Academic partnership longitudinal study; health system records | Academic research partnership established; comparison group data accessible |
| OVI 5.2: Measurable reduction in caregiver burden scale scores in longitudinal cohort, Year 3–5 | Validated caregiver burden instrument (e.g. Zarit Burden Interview); research partnership | Longitudinal follow-up feasible with Year 1 beta cohort as baseline |
| OVI 5.3: VALID sustains revenue exceeding operating costs from Year 2 onwards without grant dependency (US/global track) | Audited financial statements | Market does not reject product; no catastrophic competition |
| OVI 5.4: Kenya pilot demonstrates statistically significant improvement in mental health communication skill scores in Kenyan informal supporters (Year 3 Kenya RCT) | Peer-reviewed study; Kenyan academic partner data | Kenya partner + Shamiri + funding secured; RCT feasible |

### LEVEL 4 — PURPOSE (Specific Objective)

**Purpose Statement:** People who play VALID build durable, instinctive mental health communication skills that they apply in real-world situations within 30 days.

| Objectively Verifiable Indicators | Means of Verification | Assumptions |
|---|---|---|
| OVI 4.1: 60%+ of beta participants show improvement in validated pre/post response quality score | Beta test dataset; validated assessment instrument (Phase 3 Weeks 1–4) | Assessment instrument is sensitive enough to detect change; beta sample is representative |
| OVI 4.2: 40%+ of beta participants self-report applying a new communication response in a real-world situation within 30 days | 30-day follow-up survey (n=30 minimum) | Participants have real-world exposure to mental health situations within 30 days |
| OVI 4.3: Skill retention at 30 days is within 20% of immediate post-play score | 30-day follow-up assessment vs. post-play baseline | Participants do not engage in confounding training between sessions |
| OVI 4.4: 70%+ of beta participants report they would recommend VALID to someone in their situation | Post-play Net Promoter survey | Survey response rate ≥ 80% |
| OVI 4.5: Professional version CE participants demonstrate improved clinical response quality on post-training evaluation | CE body evaluation framework; participant self-assessment | CE accreditation obtained; professional version available for beta testing |
